# Downtime_Manufacturing_Dashboard

# Project Overview:
This dashboard is designed to analyze and visualize manufacturing downtime, providing critical insights into operational efficiency. It helps identify the main causes of downtime, track key performance indicators related to production, and monitor operator performance to optimize manufacturing processes.

# Key Metrics / Insights:
Top Downtime Factor: Identifies the most significant reasons contributing to production stoppages.
Top Operator Name (by Count): Highlights operators associated with the highest number of downtime incidents.
Operating Hours (H:M): Total hours of operation, presented in hours and minutes.
Productive Hours (H:M): Hours during which production was active and efficient.
% Productive Time: Percentage of total operating time spent in productive activities.
Downtime Hours (H:M): Total hours lost due to downtime, presented in hours and minutes.
Stoppage Rate: Frequency of production stoppages.
Availability: Measure of the operational readiness of equipment or systems.
Batches: Number of production batches processed.
Downtime % Product: Percentage of downtime attributed to specific products.
Downtimes: Total count of downtime incidents.
Product/Flavor: Breakdown of downtime by specific products and their variations.
Operator Performance: Analysis of individual operator contributions to downtime or productivity.

# Tools & Technologies:
Power BI: Utilized for data visualization, interactive dashboard creation, and reporting.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Employed for complex calculations and custom measures to derive insights from raw data.
Custom Visuals: The dashboard incorporates custom Power BI visuals such as Circular Gauge, Activity Gauge, and Advanced Gauge to enhance data representation and user experience.
